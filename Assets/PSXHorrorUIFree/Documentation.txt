PSX Horror UI Free

Documentation. A pixel-art UGUI kit: 1 theme (Fog), 9 icons and a pixel font.

  Prefabs/<Theme>/     a prefab per control: Button (and MenuItem, SaveSlot), Checkbox, Radio,
                       Slider, Bar (and FineBar, CautionBar, DangerBar, PoisonBar), Scrollbar,
                       ScrollView, InputField, Dropdown, Tabs, Tooltip, Panel, Window (and
                       InsetPanel, ItemSlot, ItemSlotSelected, ItemSlotEquipped, ExaminePanel,
                       DialoguePanel, MonitorPanel, DocumentPanel, MapRoom, MapRoomItems,
                       MapRoomCurrent, MapRoomUnknown), Label (and Title, Ink, Heading, Hint,
                       Headline, Count, Fine, Caution, Danger, Poison), Separator; Marks/<name> for
                       each of its marks
  Themes/<Theme>.asset the theme's prefabs by name (PixelTheme), for code that builds or reskins UI
  Sprites/             every piece as a sprite, 9-sliced (borders set), point filtered, uncompressed
  Font/                the fonts as BMFont and as the TextMesh Pro assets Ribbon TMP.asset, Ribbon
                       2x TMP.asset
  Demo/PSXHorrorUIFreeDemo.unity   the demo, built from the prefabs in code; the buttons along the
                       bottom swap the theme

Put a prefab under a Canvas: the prefabs lay themselves out, padded as the theme draws them, so they
stack in rows and columns. Give the canvas PixelCanvas, which scales it by the largest whole number
that fits the art height you set, so the pixels stay square (the sprites are 100 pixels per unit:
an art pixel is a canvas unit). The text is crisp at font size 9 and its multiples.

PixelButton is a Button that also sets its label's and icon's colour and its padding per state;
PixelLabel draws a text's pixel shadow; PixelBar fills a bar; PixelTabs shows a page per tab;
a TooltipArea shows its text in its canvas's Tooltip.

Needs Unity 6.3 or later and TextMesh Pro's Essential Resources (Window > TextMeshPro > Import
TMP Essential Resources; Unity offers it when a TMP text first shows).
